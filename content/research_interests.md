#### Conceptual reasoning
Language, decision making, theory of mind and a myriad of other cognitive processes rely on conceptual, or semantic, knowledge. I use behavioral and neural (fMRI and intracranial recordings) measures and computational modeling to characterize this reasoning process in humans and AI systems.

#### Language and cognition
Language interacts with many cognitive systems. To better understand language processing in the human mind and its relationship to other phenomena, I use neural (fMRI and intracranial recording) and computational modeling to study linguistic and non-linguistic representations that carry meaning.
